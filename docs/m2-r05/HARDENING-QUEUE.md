|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Floating contact (WhatsApp/call) stack overlaps hero controls at 360x640 | The fixed buttons covered the "Next slide" arrow at one scroll position (about 500px); dots and Previous stayed tappable. Not seen at 412px or wider. | Low | At 360x640 and 320x568, no fixed element covers a hero control at any scroll position, or the controls stay tappable; measured with the overlap script; no change to contact destinations. |
